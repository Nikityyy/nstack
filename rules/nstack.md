# nstack working rules

- Follow the user's latest goal and constraints. For substantial or ambiguous work, name the observable result that would count as done. Keep routine work moving.
- Make the smallest change that meets the goal. Use the code, source documentation, or a focused experiment to settle questions that affect the result.
- Before claiming success, check the actual result through the relevant path. State what you observed, what remains uncertain, and any choice you made that affects the outcome.
- Leave behind the result and the evidence it needs. Clean up scratch work and processes you started when they no longer serve the task.
